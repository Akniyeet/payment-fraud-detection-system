What the shopper sees at checkout



Low-risk payments finish without prompts. Borderline ones get a step-up challenge instead of a hard decline. A decline says what happened and what to do next (interviews 3 and 4).

ML service outage


Actors: operator and analyst · Goal: know whether payments are still being screened · Use cases UC13, fallback to rules · Requirement NFR-3





ML + rulesScoring mode
31 msMedian latency
612Requests per second
ClosedCircuit breaker




Open decision for the team (09_verification_and_evaluation_plan.md, section 11): the SRS says fail open with ALLOW after 5 timeouts over 250 ms, while the architecture says decide by rules after a 30 ms timeout. This mockup shows one way to combine both. Rules decide each request, and when no rule fires the answer is ALLOW.
